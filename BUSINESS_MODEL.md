# VoiceCue business model

**Type:** B2B2C marketplace. Studios supply inventory, brands buy it, players opt in and get rewards.

## Revenue
- Take rate: 30% of ad spend. The studio keeps 70%.
- Studio SaaS: Indie free (up to 100k MAU), Studio $2k/month, Enterprise custom (SLA, private moderation).
- Brand pricing: CPM (awareness), CPA (coupon redemption), premium for verified-safe placements.

## Unit economics (illustrative)
1M MAU x 25% opt-in x 10 impressions/day x $8 CPM = $20k/day gross. Platform 30% = $6k/day. Studio 70% = $14k/day. A slice of the platform share funds player rewards.

## Moat
Privacy-by-design on-device processing, consent logs and compliance posture, studio-controlled moderation, network of approved brands.

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Low opt-in | Rewards, clear value, transparent controls |
| Regulation (GDPR, COPPA, wiretap laws) | On-device processing, explicit consent, 18+ only, legal review |
| Brand safety | Three-layer moderation, studio veto, blocked categories |
| Player backlash | Delay and generalise ads, visible mic indicator, one-tap delete |
